\paragraph{Dataset protocol.}
For each base-model family, we organize the data as paired train/evaluation subsets. The train side of each pair contains the samples whose permutation-based Shapley values are computed, and the matched evaluation side is used to evaluate the utility of candidate subsets. Importantly, the GenSHAP training process is performed only on the datasets labeled as \emph{Training} in Tables~\ref{tab:dataset-situation-lr} and~\ref{tab:dataset-situation-svm}. The datasets labeled as \emph{Unseen test} are held out at the dataset level: no samples, labels, graphs, or Shapley values from these datasets are used to optimize GenSHAP parameters. They are used only after training to evaluate cross-dataset generalization on previously unseen datasets.
